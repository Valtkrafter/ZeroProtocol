## What this is
- Unity 6 (6000.3.25f1, URP) third-person tactical shooter with a Gun-Fu focus. Protagonist: Shirogane Yoru (VRM model, cat ears and tail, tactical outfit).
- Concept, story, lore: [README.md](../README.md), [Story_Worldbuilding.md](Story_Worldbuilding.md), [Drehbuch_Prolog.md](Drehbuch_Prolog.md).

## Direction (decided with the user, 2026-10-20)
- Over-the-shoulder camera, Ghost of Tsushima feel, dual pistols, Gun-Fu. Shooting first, then melee, then stealth.
- Lock-on: only her body faces the target; the camera and crosshair stay free (decided 2026-10-28, replaces "without a crosshair"). Slow motion is a limited ability the player triggers (like Dead Eye). Cover: yes (duck behind walls and crates and shoot), but no sluggish cover shooter.
- The user wants something original, not a classic anime gacha game.
